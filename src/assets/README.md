# PlayBeat lifestyle artwork

`playbeat-lifestyle-v1.webp` is the website's decorative lifestyle banner. It is brand artwork, separate from the real channel logos and programme imagery.

Created with the built-in image generation tool, then encoded as WebP for web delivery without changing the composition. The original generated PNG is retained separately.

Final generation prompt:

> Create a wide 21:9 photorealistic cinematic summer entertainment banner. A turquoise tropical beach and palms in golden evening light; a metallic sports car parked on the coastal road; stylish adult friends aged 25–35 in colourful summer dresses, shirts and trousers laughing and dancing at an open-air music festival. Fashion campaign styling, joyful candid faces, vivid coral pink and electric blue party lights blending into warm amber sunset. Wide environmental composition with the beach, car, and festival all clearly visible; people are fully clothed. Premium lively holiday atmosphere, beautiful photographic detail. No text, no logos, no watermark, no UI.
